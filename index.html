gmail-local

Personal, self-hosted Gmail connector

gmail-local is a small program that runs only on its owner's own computer. It lets the owner's own AI assistants (Claude and Codex) search, read, draft and send email in the owner's own Google accounts, at the owner's request.

It is a private tool for a single person. It is not a public service, it is not offered to other users, and it has no servers of its own.
